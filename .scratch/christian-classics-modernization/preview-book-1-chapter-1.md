# The Imitation of Christ — Book 1, Chapter 1

Side-by-side sample, reviewed against the Historic Benham text. Citation markers and passage boundaries are retained.

| Passage | Original | Modernized |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | He that followeth me shall not walk in darkness,(1) saith the Lord. | Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness,(1) says the Lord. |
| 2 | These are the words of Christ; and they teach us how far we must imitate His life and character, if we seek true illumination, and deliverance from all blindness of heart. | These are the words of Christ, and they teach us how far we must imitate His life and character if we seek true illumination and deliverance from all blindness of heart. |
| 3 | Let it be our most earnest study, therefore, to dwell upon the life of Jesus Christ. | Therefore, let us make it our most earnest study to dwell on the life of Jesus Christ. |
| 4 | His teaching surpasseth all teaching of holy men, and such as have His Spirit find therein the hidden manna.(2) But there are many who, though they frequently hear the Gospel, yet feel but little longing after it, because they have not the mind of Christ. | His teaching surpasses all the teaching of holy men, and those who have His Spirit find the hidden manna in it.(2) But many people, though they often hear the Gospel, feel little longing for it because they do not have the mind of Christ. |
| 5 | He, therefore, that will fully and with true wisdom understand the words of Christ, let him strive to conform his whole life to that mind of Christ. | Therefore, anyone who wants to understand the words of Christ fully and with true wisdom should strive to conform his whole life to that mind of Christ. |
| 6 | What doth it profit thee to enter into deep discussion concerning the Holy Trinity, if thou lack humility, and be thus displeasing to the Trinity? | What good does it do you to enter into deep discussion about the Holy Trinity if you lack humility and are therefore displeasing to the Trinity? |
| 7 | For verily it is not deep words that make a man holy and upright; it is a good life which maketh a man dear to God. | For it is not deep words that make a man holy and upright; it is a good life that makes a man dear to God. |
| 8 | I had rather feel contrition than be skilful in the definition thereof. | I would rather feel contrition than be skilled in defining it. |
| 9 | If thou knewest the whole Bible, and the sayings of all the philosophers, what should all this profit thee without the love and grace of God? | If you knew the whole Bible and the sayings of all the philosophers, what good would all this do you without the love and grace of God? |
| 10 | Vanity of vanities, all is vanity, save to love God, and Him only to serve. | Vanity of vanities, all is vanity, except to love God and serve Him alone. |
| 11 | That is the highest wisdom, to cast the world behind us, and to reach forward to the heavenly kingdom. | That is the highest wisdom: to cast the world behind us and reach forward to the heavenly kingdom. |
| 12 | It is vanity then to seek after, and to trust in, the riches that shall perish. | It is vanity, then, to seek and trust in riches that will perish. |
| 13 | It is vanity, too, to covet honours, and to lift up ourselves on high. | It is vanity, too, to covet honors and to lift ourselves up on high. |
| 14 | It is vanity to follow the desires of the flesh and be led by them, for this shall bring misery at the last. | It is vanity to follow the desires of the flesh and be led by them, for this will bring misery in the end. |
| 15 | It is vanity to desire a long life, and to have little care for a good life. | It is vanity to desire a long life and to care little for a good life. |
| 16 | It is vanity to take thought only for the life which now is, and not to look forward to the things which shall be hereafter. | It is vanity to concern ourselves only with the present life and not look forward to the things that are to come. |
| 17 | It is vanity to love that which quickly passeth away, and not to hasten where eternal joy abideth. | It is vanity to love what quickly passes away and not hurry to where eternal joy remains. |
| 18 | Be ofttimes mindful of the saying,(3) The eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear with hearing. | Remember often the saying,(3) The eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear with hearing. |
| 19 | Strive, therefore, to turn away thy heart from the love of the things that are seen, and to set it upon the things that are not seen. | Therefore, strive to turn your heart away from love of the things that are seen and set it on the things that are not seen. |
| 20 | For they who follow after their own fleshly lusts, defile the conscience, and destroy the grace of God. | For those who follow their own fleshly lusts defile the conscience and destroy the grace of God. |
