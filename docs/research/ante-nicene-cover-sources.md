# Ante-Nicene Fathers cover sources

Cross Canon’s [Early Christian Works index](../../public/church-fathers-preview/books.json) contains 140 entries from eight *Ante-Nicene Fathers* source volumes. The prefix before `:` in a work ID identifies the original volume. The other 270 entries belong to the first and second series of *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*. The current index has no `anf01` or `anf10` entries.

The images below are scans of historical anthology title pages. They are suitable as source-edition art for their included works, with a credit such as “Title page from *Ante-Nicene Fathers*, vol. II.” A work with a verified individual cover or illustration should use that image first. The title pages document the related American edition, while the app text comes from the edition named by CCEL in the work’s metadata. [CCEL source collection](https://www.ccel.org/fathers)

Robarts Library at the University of Toronto contributed each scan to the Internet Archive. The eight archive records report `NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT`. The dates in the table are printed on the pages themselves. I checked the visible volume, imprint, and date in every linked JPEG. The University of Toronto scan of volume IX is the 1906 fifth edition, with the 1896–1897 copyright dates on the facing page. The Library of Congress also lists the Buffalo set under an explicit public-domain reuse statement. [Internet Archive metadata example](https://archive.org/metadata/antenicenefather02robeuoft) · [Library of Congress set and rights](https://www.loc.gov/resource/gdc.27027909v7/?sp=7&st=text)
