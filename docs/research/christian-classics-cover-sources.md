# Public-domain cover sources for the Christian Classics

The seven work IDs and display names below come from the [Cross Canon Classics index](../../public/christian-classics-text/books.json). Each work has a visually checked historical scan with a usable title leaf or frontispiece. Several commentaries were originally published in multiple volumes. The selected image identifies the work, and its caption should credit the particular volume and printing shown.
