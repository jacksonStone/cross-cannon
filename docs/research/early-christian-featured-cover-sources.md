# Early Christian Works artwork sources

Reviewed 20 November 2026. The identifiers below match the [Early Christian Works catalog](../../public/church-fathers-preview/books.json). These are sources for individual works and two anthology volumes. The app's text may come from a different edition than the artwork.

## Confessions

Use Benozzo Gozzoli’s *St Augustine Reading the Epistle of St Paul* (1464–65), a portrait-shaped detail of the fresco in Sant’Agostino, San Gimignano. It illustrates the conversion described in *Confessions* VIII.12. Wikimedia Commons identifies its image as a faithful reproduction of public-domain art and marks it Public Domain Mark 1.0. [Augustinian Historical Institute’s account of the scene](https://www.augustiniana.be/public/?page_id=1692) · [File and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:TolleLege.jpg) · [640 × 1,093 JPEG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b6/TolleLege.jpg)

The Städel Museum’s Leopold von Bode drawing *Saint Augustine sitting and writing in a book* (1862), object SG 316 Z, is an alternative author illustration. Its record marks the image Public Domain. The Library of Congress also has an English edition from the 1860s if the design calls for a historical cover or title page. That book is in a collection the Library identifies as public domain. [Städel Museum object and rights record](https://sammlung.staedelmuseum.de/en/work/saint-augustine-sitting-and-writing-in-a-book) · [Städel JPEG](https://cdn.staedelmuseum.de/images/df/58/sg316z/thumb-xl.jpg) · [Library of Congress edition](https://www.loc.gov/item/33008864/)

For a component of an anthology, credit the scan as artwork from its source volume. Preserve the distinction between a historical binding, a historical title page, and subject artwork. Keep the actual work title as text next to each thumbnail, and retain the repository record and rights link when storing a derivative.
