# Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers cover sources

Cross Canon’s Early Christian Works library contains 270 works in these two series. Each record below matches the series and volume in the work’s source metadata. The scans show historical editions, including reprints. The volume art identifies the anthology rather than an individual work.

The linked Wikimedia Commons file records identify the scans as public domain. Records without a separate rights link have Archive metadata marked `NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT`. Each title page link points to the exact volume shown. For first-series volumes II and III, the selected Toronto scans have 1887 imprints. Other scans cataloged under the same volume numbers show a 1956 reprint.
