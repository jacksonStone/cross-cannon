# Public-domain library candidates

Researched 2026-11-11. Recommendations below are editorial judgments about useful additions beyond ANF/NPNF. Rights evidence concerns the named English editions and digital sources, not every edition of an old original, and is not a worldwide public-domain determination. No texts have been imported.

## Recommended candidates

| Work | Perspective and reader value | Edition and import evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Thomas Aquinas, Catena Aurea** | Medieval Catholic; probably the most natural extension of the Fathers collection. Organizes extracts from earlier commentators around Gospel passages, connecting existing authors to Bible reading. Some source content will overlap ANF/NPNF. | [CCEL Matthew volume](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/aquinas/catena1.html) offers HTML, ThML XML and UTF-8 text. Its [PDF](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/a/aquinas/catena1/cache/catena1.pdf) identifies the London J.G.F. and J. Rivington **1842** English edition. This historical edition is a strong U.S. PD candidate; CCEL separately restricts its PDF, so preserve edition provenance and resolve source-file terms before ingestion. Verify all four Gospels independently. |
| **Matthew Henry, Complete Commentary** | English Nonconformist/Reformed devotional exposition. Strong choice for ordinary readers wanting explanation and practical application beside Scripture. | [CrossWire MHC](https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=MHC) explicitly marks its downloadable module **Public Domain**, explains its CCEL/Logos text provenance. [CCEL volume I](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/henry/mhc1.html) also provides XML/UTF-8/HTML. |
| **John Calvin, Commentaries** | Reformed; substantial passage exposition and a major interpretive tradition beyond patristics. | [CCEL's specific electronic collection](https://www.ccel.org/c/calvin/comment3/comm_index.htm) identifies the **Calvin Translation Society** edition, offers volume HTML downloads, and expressly permits use for any purpose as public domain. [CrossWire CalvinCommentaries](https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=CalvinCommentaries) independently declares its module PD. Not a commentary on every biblical book. Avoid confusing this with CCEL's separate 1958 Haroutunian anthology. |
| **John Wesley, Notes on the Bible** | Methodist/Arminian; concise notes give a useful counterpoint to Calvin and Henry. | [CrossWire Wesley](https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=Wesley) explicitly declares its downloadable English commentary module **Public Domain**, identifying Sulu Kelley and Bill Brown's electronic edition as its basis. |
| **Keil and Delitzsch, Biblical Commentary on the Old Testament** | German Lutheran scholarship; more grammatical and historical depth for the Hebrew Bible. A historical scholarly resource rather than a substitute for current scholarship. | [CrossWire KD](https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=KD) identifies the nineteenth-century German/English set, its digital provenance and a facsimile, and explicitly marks the module **Public Domain**. |
| **C. H. Spurgeon, The Treasury of David** | Baptist; focused exposition of the Psalms with devotional and preaching value. A manageable addition before importing large general sermon collections. | [CrossWire TDavid](https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=TDavid) identifies the seven-volume work completed in **1885** and explicitly declares its downloadable module **Public Domain**. |
| **Thomas Aquinas, Summa Theologica** | Medieval Catholic theology; strong topical search material on God, virtues, law, Christ and sacraments. Structure by part/question/article matters more than chapters. | Gutenberg's **Complete American Edition**, translated by the Fathers of the English Dominican Province: [Part I #17611](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17611) and [Part II-II #18755](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/18755) explicitly state **Public domain in the USA** and offer HTML/plain text/EPUB. Catalog and verify remaining parts separately; do not describe a partial import as complete. |
| **Thomas a Kempis, The Imitation of Christ** | Medieval Catholic/Devotio Moderna; accessible devotional reading on humility, prayer and following Christ, useful beyond commentary. | [Gutenberg #1653](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1653), **William Benham** translation, explicitly states **Public domain in the USA**; HTML/plain text/EPUB downloads. This is a verified edition, unlike unspecified modern translations. |
| **Josephus, Antiquities and Jewish War** | First-century Jewish historical background, not Christian commentary or Scripture. Supports research on the setting of the New Testament. | **William Whiston** English translation: Gutenberg [Antiquities #2848](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2848) and [War #2850](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2850) explicitly state **Public domain in the USA**, with HTML/plain text/EPUB. Keep author perspective and historical genre visible. |
| **Teresa of Avila, Interior Castle** | Catholic Carmelite; a major work of contemplative spirituality and a valuable female author absent from many old theological corpora. | [CCEL title page](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/teresa/castle2.titlepage.html) identifies **Benedictines of Stanbrook**, revised/annotated by **Benedict Zimmerman**, Thomas Baker, **1921**, third edition. [Work page](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/teresa/castle2.html) provides XML/UTF-8/HTML. Strong historical U.S. PD edition candidate; distinguish source-file terms and avoid silently substituting a later translation. |

## Import practicality

[HelloAO's live commentary catalog](https://bible.helloao.org/api/available_commentaries.json) provides JSON access and public-domain markers for Henry, Calvin, Keil–Delitzsch, Adam Clarke, Jamieson–Fausset–Brown and John Gill. This is attractive for a verse-linked pilot. Its current coverage differs from the titles' apparent scope: Henry reports **65 books / 1,167 chapters**, Calvin **48 / 817**, KD **39 / 909**. Audit chapter/verse coverage, missing material, ranges, text direction and source provenance before import; several English entries unexpectedly carry `textDirection: rtl`. Its Tyndale notes are **CC BY-SA 4.0**, not public domain.

Suggested first batch: Catena Aurea, Henry, Wesley and Treasury of David for distinct passage-study experiences; then Calvin/KD for depth and Imitation/Josephus for genre breadth. Display tradition, author, date, translator and source edition. Keep commentary and historical material distinguishable from canonical texts in search.

## Valuable leads with edition traps

- **Philo, Works**: [CCEL title page](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/philo/works.i.html) identifies **C. D. Yonge**, London H. G. Bohn, **1854–1890**. [Work catalog](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/philo/works) offers XML and ASCII text. Strong later historical-context candidate; verify the precise historical text and digital terms rather than modern revised Yonge editions. Philo belongs with Jewish background literature.
- **Creeds and confessions**: excellent small additions for comparing traditions, but choose a verified earlier edition or authorized translation. [CCEL Schaff volume I](https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds1.all.html) contains David S. Schaff's **1931** revised-edition preface and added material. An ancient creed's age does not establish the status of its English translation or 1931 apparatus. Do not automatically treat the entire CCEL edition as the 1877 original.
- **Brother Lawrence**: avoid treating [Gutenberg #5657](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5657) as PD. The catalog explicitly says **Copyrighted**, and its [text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/5657/pg5657-images.html) identifies Lightheart's copyrighted **2002** edition. Find a separately verified historical English edition instead.
- **John of the Cross**: a good author, but [CCEL Dark Night](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/john_cross/dark_night.i.html) uses E. Allison Peers' third revised edition. [Penn's Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupname?key=John+of+the+Cross%2C+Saint%2C+1542-1591) identifies earlier David Lewis/Zimmerman **1908/1916** and Gabriela Cunninghame Graham **1905** scans. Prefer verifying one of those instead of assuming any English Dark Night is PD.
