# Public-domain artwork for the Cross Canon library

The [cover inventory](library-cover-inventory.json) maps every work ID in the two current library indexes to an artwork source. It covers 410 Early Christian Works and seven Christian Classics, plus the Bible cover shared by the Scripture picker. The sources were reviewed on 20 November 2026.

| Collection | Artwork coverage | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bible | Existing Cross Canon cover based on the sixth-century Sinai icon. The individual books are destinations in the Bible picker. | [Cover and artwork credits](../../assets/kindle/README.md) |
| Early Christian Works | Existing First Clement cover, work-specific art for *Confessions*, and public-domain historical scans of all 36 source volumes for the anthology entries. | [Featured works](early-christian-featured-cover-sources.md) · [Ante-Nicene Fathers](ante-nicene-cover-sources.md) · [Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers](nicene-cover-sources.md) |
| Christian Classics | A title leaf or frontispiece from a historical printing for all seven works. The notes identify the particular printing or volume shown. | [Classics source records](christian-classics-cover-sources.md) |

A large portion of Early Christian Works consists of letters, homilies, and treatises included in larger volumes. Their assigned image is edition artwork from the corresponding anthology. The display should identify it as such. The work’s title and author stay in text next to the image. The featured-works notes also record individual alternatives for works such as *City of God* and *The Pastor of Hermas*.

The illustrated mobile preview uses the existing Bible and First Clement assets, Gozzoli’s fresco of Augustine’s conversion, and the frontispiece from the 1886 Benham edition of *The Imitation of Christ*. The last two images are now stored locally with credits. [Local artwork credits](../../assets/library-covers/README.md)
